Lassi Vapaakallio

vapaalassi@gmail.com

I've been making games since 2013, with 40 finished projects at the latest count. Since Autumn 2015 I've been making games full-time, first as a student, then as a professional from 2017 onwards.

I have strong skills in both Game Design and Programming, with a BSc in Computer Science and an MA in Game Design & Production to back them up.

My favourite thing is talking with team members, coming up with ideas and testing them as quickly as possible. My ideal position is a designer/programmer hybrid, working in a collaborative small team.
